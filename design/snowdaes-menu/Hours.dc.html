Open hours · four treatments

Seven rows is the lazy answer

Nobody opens a dessert shop's website to read a timetable. They want one of two answers: can I go now, or when can I go. Each option below answers those in less room than the table does.

A · TODAY
{{d.day}} {{d.hours}}
392px of screen to say one thing. No live status — the visitor does the arithmetic. Doesn't scale to two shops.
B · RIBBON ✦
Open now · closes 9 PM
{{d.day}} {{d.close}}
Every day from 12:00 PM
72px. Live status first, whole week still visible, today ringed. Opens from 12 every day, so only the close time varies — the ribbon shows just that.
C · SENTENCE
Open till 9
Tomorrow 12–9. Late Fri & Sat till 10.
Smallest and warmest. Reads like a person talking. Hides the full week behind a tap — fine on mobile, thin if someone is planning Saturday.
D · CLOCK BAR
3 hrs left today
12 PMNOW 6:029 PM
Kitchen stops egg puffs 15 min before close
Most urgent. Turns hours into a reason to leave now. Risky: it can read as pressure, and it says nothing about tomorrow.
B is in Main. It is the only one that answers both questions at once, and the only one that survives having two shops — Lowell and Billerica each get their own ribbon from their own Clover feed, and a closed day greys out rather than disappearing. C is the better footer treatment. D is worth trying on the cart screen, where urgency is already the point.
