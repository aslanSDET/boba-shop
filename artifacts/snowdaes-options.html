What the discount actually does

A customer types NEWCUSTOMER and 10% comes off. That's the whole feature, and Clover cannot do it at all.

  1. The price comes down before tax

    $11.95 becomes $10.75, then tax on the lower number — $11.50 to pay. Same way you'd do it at the counter.

  2. Clover records it as a real discount

    Your reports say "NEWCUSTOMER 10%", not a mystery lower price. So the books still make sense at the end of the month.

  3. You know who used it

    Which is the actual point. A code on a flyer, a code for students, a code for people who haven't been in for a month.
